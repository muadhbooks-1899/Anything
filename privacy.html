A quick note: This is a starter policy for a simple demo website, not legal advice. Update it to reflect your actual tools, data practices, and local legal requirements before publishing.

01

Information we collect

Anything may receive information you choose to share with us, such as your name, email address, and message when you contact us. Messages sent through our contact form are securely stored by our hosting provider, Netlify, so we can read and reply to them.

Depending on how you deploy and configure this website, your hosting provider may process basic technical information such as IP address, browser type, and server logs.

02

How we use information

If you contact us, we would use the details you provide to respond to your message and follow up on your request. We do not sell personal information. Any additional uses should be explained here before you launch.

03

Cookies and similar tools

This template does not intentionally set analytics or advertising cookies. Hosting platforms, embedded content, or third-party tools you add later may use cookies or similar technologies. Document those tools here and provide any notices or consent controls required by law.

04

When information is shared

We may need service providers to help operate the website, such as a hosting provider or email service, if you configure those services. We would share only what is reasonably needed for their role. We may also disclose information when required by law.

05

Keeping information safe

Reasonable safeguards should be used to protect information, but no website or method of transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Avoid submitting sensitive personal information through a general contact form.

06

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information. To make a request, contact us using the details on our contact page. We may need to verify your request before responding.

07

Questions about privacy?

If you have a question about this policy or how information is handled, please get in touch. Replace this generic policy with one reviewed for your actual website before going live.
